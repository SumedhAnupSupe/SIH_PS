Incident Date: 2026-01-15
Incident ID: INC-2026-001
Location: Substation Alpha, Bay 7
Worker Count: 4
Injury Severity: Near Miss

Incident Description:

The crew was scheduled to replace a transformer before the outage window ended. The work was part of a planned maintenance outage that had been scheduled three months in advance.

During the work, unexpected rain began. The crew discussed the weather conditions but decided to continue because delaying the work would extend the outage by an additional 12 hours. The supervisor noted that the crew had completed similar transformer replacements many times previously.

The revised weather conditions were not included in the original pre-task plan. The crew did not perform a formal reassessment of the hazards after the rain started. No updated safety briefing was conducted to address the new wet conditions.

The electrical hazard was recognized by the lead worker, who noted that the wet surfaces increased the risk of arc flash. However, the crew continued working despite the increased hazard. The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before.

No stop-work authority was exercised. The crew did not call for a safety stand-down to discuss the changed conditions. One junior worker mentioned concern about the wet conditions but was told by the supervisor that they had done this many times before and it would be fine.

The pre-task plan had been completed before the work began, but it did not account for potential weather changes. The original plan was based on dry conditions only. No revised plan was developed after the rain started.

Equipment involved: 500kV transformer, crane, electrical testing equipment. The crane operator noted that the wet ground was making the crane less stable but continued operations.

The crew was under pressure to complete the work before the outage window closed. The outage coordinator had communicated that any delay would affect the restoration schedule for approximately 15,000 customers.

After the incident, the crew completed the transformer replacement without further incident. The near-miss was reported the following day during the post-job safety review.
