Injury Severity: First Aid

Incident Description:
Welding crew performed hot work on a floating roof crude oil storage tank. The hot work permit was obtained but incomplete. Vapor hazard was not recognized before hot work commenced. Stop-work authority not exercised when vapors detected. No revised plan when gas monitor indicated LEL increase. Hot work permit requirements were not fully met. Work proceeded despite changing wind direction carrying vapors. Pre-task plan did not address continuous gas monitoring requirement. Crew assumed tank was not safe because it had been cleaned previously.

The crew had adequate time to complete the work and there was no schedule pressure. 

Immediate Actions Taken:
Fire watch was posted but no gas monitoring was performed during work. Work continued when vapors detected.
