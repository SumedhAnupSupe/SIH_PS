Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a process vessel for repair. The confined space permit was obtained and valid. No plan was in place for changing atmospheric conditions. Confined space entry procedures were not followed. Stop-work authority was not exercised when gas tester alarmed. The atmospheric hazard was not not recognized before entry. Entry was not made without the standard gas testing procedure.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. Life-Saving Rule LSR03 (No attendant posted outside confined space entry.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
