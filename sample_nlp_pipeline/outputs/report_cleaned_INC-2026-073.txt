Injury Severity: Fatality

Incident Description:
Excavation crew using trencher near buried water injection pipeline. The excavation permit was expired. Stop-work authority not exercised when pipeline locate marks unclear. Crew assumed pipeline was deeper based on old drawings. Hand digging verification not performed near pipeline. Excavation permit requirements not fully met. No revised plan when pipeline proximity identified. Pipeline hazard not recognized in excavation planning. Unexpected soil conditions encountered during excavation.

The operator was experienced but the ground conditions were different from the drawings. Life-Saving Rule LSR06 (Excavator operated without spotter near pipeline.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Excavation continued despite proximity to pipeline. No hand digging verification performed.
