Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a storage tank for valve replacement. The confined space permit was obtained but incomplete. Entry was made without the standard gas testing procedure. Confined space entry procedures were not followed. No plan was in place for changing atmospheric conditions. The atmospheric hazard was not recognized before entry. Stop-work authority was not exercised when gas tester alarmed. The safe work procedure for confined space entry was not followed. The crew was under pressure to complete the maintenance before shift end.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. Life-Saving Rule LSR03 (No attendant posted outside confined space entry.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
