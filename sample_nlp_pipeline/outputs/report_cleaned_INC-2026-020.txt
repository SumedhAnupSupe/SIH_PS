Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a separator for valve replacement. The confined space permit was expired. Confined space entry procedures were not followed. Entry was made without the standard gas testing procedure. The safe work procedure for confined space entry was not followed. The atmospheric hazard was not not recognized before entry. The crew was not under pressure to complete the maintenance before shift end.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. 

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
