Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a storage tank for cleaning. The confined space permit was obtained and valid. No plan was in place for changing atmospheric conditions. The atmospheric hazard was not recognized before entry. The safe work procedure for confined space entry was not followed. Entry was made without the standard gas testing procedure. The crew was not under pressure to complete the maintenance before shift end. Confined space entry procedures were not not followed.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. 

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
