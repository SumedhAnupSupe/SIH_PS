Incident Description:
The crew was scheduled to replace a power transformer before the outage window ended. During the work, heavy rain began. The electrical hazard was recognized by the lead worker, who noted that the wet surfaces increased the risk of arc flash. One junior worker mentioned concern about the wet conditions but was told by the supervisor that they had done this many times before and it would be fine. The revised weather conditions were not included in the original pre-task plan. No stop-work authority was not exercised. The electrical hazard was not recognized by the lead worker, who noted that the wet surfaces increased the risk of arc flash.

The crew was under pressure to complete the work before the outage window closed. Life-Saving Rule LSR02 (Energy isolation was not verified before starting work on the transformer.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
The crew continued working despite the increased hazard. No stop-work authority was exercised.
