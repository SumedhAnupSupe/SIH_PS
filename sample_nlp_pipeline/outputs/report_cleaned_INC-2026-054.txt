Incident Description:
Excavation crew using trencher near buried natural gas pipeline. The excavation permit was not obtained. Stop-work authority not exercised when pipeline locate marks unclear. Excavation permit requirements not fully met. Unexpected soil conditions encountered during excavation. Pipeline hazard not recognized in excavation planning. Hand digging verification not performed near pipeline. Crew assumed pipeline was not deeper based on old drawings.

The operator was experienced but the ground conditions were different from the drawings. 

Immediate Actions Taken:
Excavation continued despite proximity to pipeline. No hand digging verification performed.
