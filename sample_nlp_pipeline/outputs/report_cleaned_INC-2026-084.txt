Incident Description:
Excavation crew using backhoe near buried water injection pipeline. The excavation permit was obtained but incomplete. Stop-work authority not exercised when pipeline locate marks unclear. Pipeline hazard not recognized in excavation planning. Crew assumed pipeline was deeper based on old drawings. Hand digging verification not performed near pipeline. Unexpected soil conditions encountered during excavation. No revised plan when pipeline proximity identified. Excavation permit requirements not fully met.

The operator was experienced but the ground conditions were different from the drawings. Life-Saving Rule LSR03 (Workers in line of fire of potential pipeline strike.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Excavation continued despite proximity to pipeline. No hand digging verification performed.
