Incident Description:
Excavation crew using trencher near buried natural gas pipeline. The excavation permit was obtained and valid. Stop-work authority not exercised when pipeline locate marks unclear. Pipeline hazard not recognized in excavation planning. No revised plan when pipeline proximity identified. Crew assumed pipeline was deeper based on old drawings. Unexpected soil conditions encountered during excavation. Hand digging verification not performed near pipeline. Excavation permit requirements not fully met.

The operator was experienced but the ground conditions were different from the drawings. 

Immediate Actions Taken:
Excavation continued despite proximity to pipeline. No hand digging verification performed.
