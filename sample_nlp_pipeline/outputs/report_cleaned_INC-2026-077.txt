Injury Severity: Lost Time Injury

Incident Description:
Excavation crew using backhoe near buried crude oil pipeline. The excavation permit was obtained and valid. Crew assumed pipeline was deeper based on old drawings. Pipeline hazard not recognized in excavation planning. Hand digging verification not performed near pipeline. Stop-work authority not exercised when pipeline locate marks unclear. Unexpected soil conditions encountered during excavation. No revised plan when pipeline proximity identified. Excavation permit requirements not fully met.

The operator was experienced but the ground conditions were different from the drawings. Life-Saving Rule LSR07 (Pipeline locator not calibrated/verified before use.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Excavation continued despite proximity to pipeline. No hand digging verification performed.
