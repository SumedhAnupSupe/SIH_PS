Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a storage tank for cleaning. The confined space permit was expired. Stop-work authority was not exercised when gas tester alarmed. The safe work procedure for confined space entry was not followed. Entry was made without the standard gas testing procedure. The atmospheric hazard was not recognized before entry. Confined space entry procedures were not followed. The crew was not under pressure to complete the maintenance before shift end. No plan was not in place for changing atmospheric conditions.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. Life-Saving Rule LSR02 (Energy isolation not verified for connected piping.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
