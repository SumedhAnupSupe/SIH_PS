Incident Description:

The revised weather conditions were not included in the original pre-task plan. The supervisor noted that the crew had completed similar transformer replacements many times previously. The crew was not under pressure to complete the work before the outage window closed. The electrical hazard was not recognized by the lead worker, who noted that the wet surfaces increased the risk of arc flash.
