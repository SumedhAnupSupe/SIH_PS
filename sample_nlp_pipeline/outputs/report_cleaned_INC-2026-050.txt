Incident Description:
Welding crew performed hot work on a fixed roof crude oil storage tank. The hot work permit was obtained and valid. Crew assumed tank was safe because it had been cleaned previously. No revised plan when gas monitor indicated LEL increase. Vapor hazard was not recognized before hot work commenced. Stop-work authority not exercised when vapors detected. Work proceeded despite changing wind direction carrying vapors. Pre-task plan did not address continuous gas monitoring requirement. Hot work permit requirements were not not fully met.

The crew had adequate time to complete the work and there was no schedule pressure. Life-Saving Rule LSR07 (Gas monitor not calibrated before use.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Fire watch was posted but no gas monitoring was performed during work. Work continued when vapors detected.
