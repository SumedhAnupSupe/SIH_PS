Incident Description:
Excavation crew using backhoe near buried produced water pipeline. The excavation permit was obtained but incomplete. Pipeline hazard not recognized in excavation planning. No revised plan when pipeline proximity identified. Hand digging verification not performed near pipeline. Unexpected soil conditions encountered during excavation. Excavation permit requirements not fully met. Crew assumed pipeline was not deeper based on old drawings.

The operator was experienced but the ground conditions were different from the drawings. Life-Saving Rule LSR07 (Pipeline locator not calibrated/verified before use.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Excavation continued despite proximity to pipeline. No hand digging verification performed.
