Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a process vessel for cleaning. The confined space permit was expired. Stop-work authority was not exercised when gas tester alarmed. Confined space entry procedures were not followed. The crew was under pressure to complete the maintenance before shift end. The safe work procedure for confined space entry was not followed. No plan was not in place for changing atmospheric conditions. Entry was not made without the standard gas testing procedure.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. Life-Saving Rule LSR04 (Confined space entered without valid permit and atmospheric testing.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
