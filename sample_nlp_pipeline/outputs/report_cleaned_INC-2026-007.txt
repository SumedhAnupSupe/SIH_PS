Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a separator for valve replacement. The confined space permit was obtained and valid. Confined space entry procedures were not followed. The atmospheric hazard was not recognized before entry. No plan was in place for changing atmospheric conditions. The crew was under pressure to complete the maintenance before shift end. Stop-work authority was not not exercised when gas tester alarmed. The safe work procedure for confined space entry was not not followed.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. Life-Saving Rule LSR03 (No attendant posted outside confined space entry.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
