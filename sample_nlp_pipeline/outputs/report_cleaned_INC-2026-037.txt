Injury Severity: Lost Time Injury

Incident Description:
Excavation crew using trencher near buried crude oil pipeline. The excavation permit was not obtained. Pipeline hazard not recognized in excavation planning. Crew assumed pipeline was deeper based on old drawings. Stop-work authority not exercised when pipeline locate marks unclear. No revised plan when pipeline proximity identified. Excavation permit requirements not fully met. Unexpected soil conditions encountered during excavation. Hand digging verification not performed near pipeline.

The operator was experienced but the ground conditions were different from the drawings. Life-Saving Rule LSR06 (Excavator operated without spotter near pipeline.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Excavation continued despite proximity to pipeline. No hand digging verification performed.
