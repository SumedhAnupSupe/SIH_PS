Incident Description:
Excavation crew using backhoe near buried water injection pipeline. The excavation permit was expired. Pipeline hazard not recognized in excavation planning. Unexpected soil conditions encountered during excavation. Excavation permit requirements not fully met. No revised plan when pipeline proximity identified. Hand digging verification not performed near pipeline. Crew assumed pipeline was not deeper based on old drawings.

The operator was experienced but the ground conditions were different from the drawings. Life-Saving Rule LSR03 (Workers in line of fire of potential pipeline strike.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Excavation continued despite proximity to pipeline. No hand digging verification performed.
