Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a separator for cleaning. The confined space permit was obtained but incomplete. Stop-work authority was not exercised when gas tester alarmed. No plan was in place for changing atmospheric conditions. The atmospheric hazard was not recognized before entry. Confined space entry procedures were not followed. The crew was not under pressure to complete the maintenance before shift end. The safe work procedure for confined space entry was not not followed.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. Life-Saving Rule LSR03 (No attendant posted outside confined space entry.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
