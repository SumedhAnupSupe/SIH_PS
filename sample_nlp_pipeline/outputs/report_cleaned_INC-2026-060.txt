Incident Description:
The crew was scheduled to replace a distribution transformer before the outage window ended. During the work, heavy rain began. The electrical hazard was recognized by the lead worker, who noted that the wet surfaces increased the risk of arc flash. One junior worker mentioned concern about the wet conditions but was told by the supervisor that they had done this many times before and it would be fine. Unexpected rain began during the transformer replacement. The crew was under pressure to complete the work before the outage window closed. No revised plan was developed after the rain started. No stop-work authority was exercised. The supervisor noted that the crew had completed similar transformer replacements many times previously. The revised weather conditions were not included in the original pre-task plan. The electrical hazard was not recognized by the lead worker, who noted that the wet surfaces increased the risk of arc flash.

The crew was under pressure to complete the work before the outage window closed. Life-Saving Rule LSR03 (Workers positioned themselves in the line of fire during crane operations.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
The crew continued working despite the increased hazard. No stop-work authority was exercised.
