Incident Description:
Welding crew performed hot work on a floating roof crude oil storage tank. The hot work permit was obtained and valid. Vapor hazard was not recognized before hot work commenced. Crew assumed tank was safe because it had been cleaned previously. Stop-work authority not exercised when vapors detected. Hot work permit requirements were not fully met. Pre-task plan did not address continuous gas monitoring requirement. No revised plan when gas monitor indicated LEL increase.

The crew had adequate time to complete the work and there was no schedule pressure. Life-Saving Rule LSR05 (Hot work performed without proper fire blankets and spark containment.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Fire watch was posted but no gas monitoring was performed during work. Work continued when vapors detected.
