Incident Description:
Welding crew performed hot work on a fixed roof crude oil storage tank. The hot work permit was expired. Hot work permit requirements were not fully met. Work proceeded despite changing wind direction carrying vapors. Vapor hazard was not recognized before hot work commenced. Crew assumed tank was not safe because it had been cleaned previously. Stop-work authority not exercised when vapors detected.

The crew had adequate time to complete the work and there was no schedule pressure. Life-Saving Rule LSR02 (Adjacent piping not isolated and de-energized.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Fire watch was posted but no gas monitoring was performed during work. Work continued when vapors detected.
