Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a storage tank for repair. The confined space permit was not obtained. Confined space entry procedures were not followed. The crew was under pressure to complete the maintenance before shift end. The atmospheric hazard was not not recognized before entry. Entry was not made without the standard gas testing procedure.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. 

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
