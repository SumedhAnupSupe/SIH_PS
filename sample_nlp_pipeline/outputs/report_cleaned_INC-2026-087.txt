Incident Description:
Excavation crew using backhoe near buried natural gas pipeline. The excavation permit was not obtained. Excavation permit requirements not fully met. Hand digging verification not performed near pipeline. Crew assumed pipeline was deeper based on old drawings. No revised plan when pipeline proximity identified. Unexpected soil conditions encountered during excavation. Stop-work authority not exercised when pipeline locate marks unclear. Pipeline hazard not recognized in excavation planning.

The operator was experienced but the ground conditions were different from the drawings. Life-Saving Rule LSR03 (Workers in line of fire of potential pipeline strike.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Excavation continued despite proximity to pipeline. No hand digging verification performed.
