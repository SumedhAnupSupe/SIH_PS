Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a heat exchanger for inspection. The confined space permit was obtained but incomplete. The crew was under pressure to complete the maintenance before shift end. The atmospheric hazard was not recognized before entry. Entry was made without the standard gas testing procedure. Stop-work authority was not exercised when gas tester alarmed. The safe work procedure for confined space entry was not followed. No plan was not in place for changing atmospheric conditions. Confined space entry procedures were not not followed.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. Life-Saving Rule LSR02 (Energy isolation not verified for connected piping.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
