Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a heat exchanger for repair. The confined space permit was not obtained. Confined space entry procedures were not followed. Stop-work authority was not exercised when gas tester alarmed. The crew was under pressure to complete the maintenance before shift end. The atmospheric hazard was not recognized before entry. Entry was made without the standard gas testing procedure. No plan was not in place for changing atmospheric conditions. The safe work procedure for confined space entry was not not followed.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. Life-Saving Rule LSR03 (No attendant posted outside confined space entry.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
