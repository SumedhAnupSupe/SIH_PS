Injury Severity: Near Miss

Incident Description:
Welding crew performed hot work on a floating roof crude oil storage tank. The hot work permit was obtained and valid. Stop-work authority not exercised when vapors detected. Vapor hazard was not not recognized before hot work commenced. Work proceeded despite changing wind direction carrying vapors.

The crew had adequate time to complete the work and there was no schedule pressure. Life-Saving Rule LSR02 (Adjacent piping not isolated and de-energized.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Fire watch was posted but no gas monitoring was performed during work. Work continued when vapors detected.
