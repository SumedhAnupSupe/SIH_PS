Injury Severity: Lost Time Injury

Incident Description:
Welding crew performed hot work on a floating roof crude oil storage tank. The hot work permit was not obtained. Stop-work authority not exercised when vapors detected. Work proceeded despite changing wind direction carrying vapors. Hot work permit requirements were not fully met. Pre-task plan did not address continuous gas monitoring requirement. Vapor hazard was not recognized before hot work commenced. No revised plan when gas monitor indicated LEL increase. Crew assumed tank was not safe because it had been cleaned previously.

The crew had adequate time to complete the work and there was no schedule pressure. Life-Saving Rule LSR02 (Adjacent piping not isolated and de-energized.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Fire watch was posted but no gas monitoring was performed during work. Work continued when vapors detected.
