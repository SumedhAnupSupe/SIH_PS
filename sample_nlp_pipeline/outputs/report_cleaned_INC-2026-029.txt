Incident Description:
Maintenance crew entered a heat exchanger for repair. The confined space permit was obtained and valid. The crew was under pressure to complete the maintenance before shift end. The safe work procedure for confined space entry was not followed. Entry was made without the standard gas testing procedure. Stop-work authority was not exercised when gas tester alarmed. No plan was in place for changing atmospheric conditions. The atmospheric hazard was not recognized before entry. Confined space entry procedures were not followed.

The supervisor stated that the team was experienced and had worked in similar conditions before. 

Immediate Actions Taken:
Work continued without proper atmospheric monitoring. No one raised concerns about the permit status.
