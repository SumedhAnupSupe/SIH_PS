Incident Description:
Technicians performed major overhaul maintenance on the steam turbine. The technicians followed the standard operating procedure and completed the pre-task safety briefing as required. Stop-work authority was exercised appropriately. Both workers were properly trained on the equipment and had several years of experience with turbine maintenance. The pre-task plan was not comprehensive and included the cooling pump inspection. The safety culture at the facility was not supportive of workers reporting concerns without fear of reprisal.

Both workers were properly trained on the equipment and had several years of experience with turbine maintenance. Life-Saving Rule LSR01 (Valid work permit obtained and verified before maintenance.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Stop-work authority was exercised appropriately. The crew halted the work and waited for the engineering team to assess the situation.
