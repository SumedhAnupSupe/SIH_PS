Incident Description:
The crew was scheduled to replace a instrument transformer before the outage window ended. During the work, high winds began. The electrical hazard was recognized by the lead worker, who noted that the wet surfaces increased the risk of arc flash. No revised plan was developed after the rain started. Unexpected rain began during the transformer replacement. The supervisor noted that the crew had completed similar transformer replacements many times previously. The revised weather conditions were not included in the original pre-task plan. The crew was not under pressure to complete the work before the outage window closed. The electrical hazard was not recognized by the lead worker, who noted that the wet surfaces increased the risk of arc flash.

The crew was under pressure to complete the work before the outage window closed. Life-Saving Rule LSR02 (Energy isolation was not verified before starting work on the transformer.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
The crew continued working despite the increased hazard. No stop-work authority was exercised.
