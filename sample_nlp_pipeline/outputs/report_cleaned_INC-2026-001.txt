Incident Description:
The crew was scheduled to replace a distribution transformer before the outage window ended. During the work, high winds began. The electrical hazard was recognized by the lead worker, who noted that the wet surfaces increased the risk of arc flash. No revised plan was developed after the rain started. The revised weather conditions were not included in the original pre-task plan. One junior worker mentioned concern about the wet conditions but was told by the supervisor that they had done this many times before and it would be fine. The electrical hazard was recognized by the lead worker, who noted that the wet surfaces increased the risk of arc flash. Unexpected rain began during the transformer replacement. The supervisor noted that the crew had completed similar transformer replacements many times previously. No stop-work authority was not exercised.

The crew was under pressure to complete the work before the outage window closed. Life-Saving Rule LSR09 (Fall protection not used while working on elevated transformer platform.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
The crew continued working despite the increased hazard. No stop-work authority was exercised.
