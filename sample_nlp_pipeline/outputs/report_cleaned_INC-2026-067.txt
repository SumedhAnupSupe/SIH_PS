Incident Description:
Excavation crew using excavator near buried crude oil pipeline. The excavation permit was expired. Pipeline hazard not recognized in excavation planning. Unexpected soil conditions encountered during excavation.

The operator was experienced but the ground conditions were different from the drawings. Life-Saving Rule LSR07 (Pipeline locator not calibrated/verified before use.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Excavation continued despite proximity to pipeline. No hand digging verification performed.
