Incident Description:
Excavation crew using backhoe near buried crude oil pipeline. The excavation permit was obtained but incomplete. Excavation permit requirements not fully met. Unexpected soil conditions encountered during excavation. Pipeline hazard not recognized in excavation planning. No revised plan when pipeline proximity identified. Stop-work authority not exercised when pipeline locate marks unclear. Crew assumed pipeline was not deeper based on old drawings. Hand digging verification not performed near pipeline.

The operator was experienced but the ground conditions were different from the drawings. Life-Saving Rule LSR07 (Pipeline locator not calibrated/verified before use.) was relevant to this activity.

Immediate Actions Taken:
Excavation continued despite proximity to pipeline. No hand digging verification performed.
